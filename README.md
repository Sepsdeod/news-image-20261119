A full-day front-page poster summing up the world's news of Thursday
19 November 2026: 36 stories across 7 regions plus global — the GTA VI
launch leads (live first in New Zealand at midnight, critics 99/100),
with the ICJ's Rohingya verdict, the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin
trilateral in Shenzhen, the third Crimea Bridge strike, the Gulf
escalation, Jeffries' 251–11 Speaker nomination, unrest in Bangladesh,
Cyclone Akvan, the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan, the UK's 1,000-drone pledge,
a record $190.4m Van Gogh sale, the US–Venezuela military pact, the
European Commission cyberattack, Ethiopia–Eritrea troop build-up,
Cyclone Xavier, Fiji's drought disaster, COP31, and more. Every story
was verified against same-day reporting (Reuters, AP, BBC, GOV.UK,
Politico, and others). Drawn from scratch with Pillow (`draw.py`);
`main.ts` copies the finished `entry.png` to the output directory,
byte for byte, every run.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
